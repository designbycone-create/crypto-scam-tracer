# Trust pledge

Fraud victims are the most-targeted people online: the second wave of crypto
fraud is the "recovery scam" — someone who promises to get your money back for
a fee. This project exists to be the opposite. These rules are part of the
product, not marketing:

1. **Free for victims, always.** The victim edition will never charge you, ask
   for a "recovery fee", a deposit, a "tax" or a "release fee".
2. **Never asks for a seed phrase, private key or wallet connection.** No
   legitimate investigator, exchange or agency ever needs them.
3. **Never contacts you first.** No DMs, no calls, no "we found your funds".
   If someone claims to represent this project and contacts you, it's a scam.
4. **Never sends anything on your behalf.** It builds drafts and kits; you (or
   your officer) decide what to submit.
5. **Never promises recovery.** It maximizes the odds of a freeze and builds
   the evidence. Freezes and returns are decided by exchanges, issuers, police
   and courts.
6. **Never guesses contact details.** A freeze request sent to a guessed
   address wastes the hours that matter. Only verified channels, with sources.
7. **Your data stays on your computer.** Case files, kits and logs are local
   and git-ignored.

Free help that is real: your local police, the FBI's IC3 (ic3.gov), SEAL 911
(Telegram @seal_911_bot), Chainabuse (chainabuse.com).
